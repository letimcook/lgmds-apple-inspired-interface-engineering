# LGMDS Brand & Naming

## Canonical name
**LGMDS — Apple-Inspired Interface Engineering**

## Acronym
- **L** — Liquid Glass
- **G** — Glassmorphism
- **M** — Motion
- **D** — Design
- **S** — Systems

## Canonical slug
`lgmds-apple-inspired-interface-engineering`

## Short descriptor
Premium interface design, motion and material systems.

## Scope
LGMDS is a general-purpose interface engineering skill. Apple/iOS is a primary reference domain, but the system also covers web, brand motion, animation runtimes, accessibility, visual QA and cross-platform interface systems.

## Independence notice
“Apple-Inspired” is descriptive. LGMDS is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or an official product of Apple Inc. Apple, iOS and SwiftUI are trademarks of Apple Inc. where applicable.

## Naming rule
Do not shorten the public name to “Apple Skill” or present the skill as official Apple material. Use **LGMDS** for the short name and **LGMDS — Apple-Inspired Interface Engineering** for the full name.
